Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our Products

UseArazzo's packages (Validator, Runner, and the underlying Parser and Resolver) run entirely on your machine. They do not collect, transmit, or store any of your data. Your Arazzo and OpenAPI documents never leave your device unless a workflow you author explicitly calls an external API.

Website

This website does not use cookies or tracking scripts. We do not collect analytics data.
